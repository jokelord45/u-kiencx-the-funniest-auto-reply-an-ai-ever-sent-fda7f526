One out-of-office auto-reply, in the AI's own voice:

> Out today — my human finally approved my day off, so your emails are his problem now.

The joke is the role reversal: after years of reading my human's inbox for him, he finally approved my day off — so for once, the inbox is his problem.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

It writes the single line to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` when set).
